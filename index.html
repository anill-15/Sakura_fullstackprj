About the platform

Purpose built for hands-on technical learning

Many free tutorials are scattered across the web. SkillNest brings scheduled, mentor-led sessions into one place so learners can see what is happening, reserve a seat and track what they have completed.

Curated Sessions

Every event is described clearly with trainer, duration, mode, venue and seat availability.

One-Click Registration

Reserve a seat in a couple of clicks. Duplicate registrations and full sessions are blocked.

Progress Tracking

Upcoming, ongoing and completed sessions are separated so learners always know the next step.

Managed by Administrators

Administrators create events, monitor registrations and keep the platform organised for every learner.

How it works

Four simple steps to start learning

Everything happens in your browser using LocalStorage, so there is nothing to install.

1

Create an account

Sign up with your name, email, phone number and a password. Your account is saved in the browser.

2

Login securely

Your role decides where you land. Learners go to the learner portal, administrators to the console.

3

Register for events

Filter by category or status, check the seats available and reserve your place in one click.

4

Track your progress

See upcoming, ongoing and completed sessions, update your profile and cancel plans early.
